## A Note on What I Can Help With

This blog documents my own setup and the decisions behind it. I am glad to clarify anything I have written, share what worked or did not work for me, and point you toward resources I found useful. What I cannot do is take responsibility for changes you make to your own systems based on what you read here. Every homelab is different, and the right choice for mine may not be the right choice for yours. Treat everything here as one person's experience rather than authoritative advice.
